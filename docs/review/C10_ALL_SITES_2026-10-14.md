## What changed vs the earlier note

- The "all clustered in the dialog/overlay region (0x4373xx/0x50d4xx)" line was
  **too strong**. Real distribution: two WndProcs (`0x4085cd`, `0x435e1c`),
  a 35-byte thunk (`0x40963d`), two sash/splitter control clusters
  (`0x40e7xx`, `0x412exx`/`0x4130xx`), and the K2 overlay cluster
  (`0x435e1c` is a WndProc, `0x436f19`/`0x437488`/`0x43799a` are overlay).
- The cursor vocabulary around capture UI is richer than "dialog cursors":
  `IDC_SIZEALL` on sash drag, per-handle resize cursors on the K2 overlay,
  a crosshair-shaped `0x7F82` on overlay miss. **None of these is the mouse-
  capture path** (C1/C3/C8/C9 chain).
- The reading does not change `mouse_capture.py`: the port's sash already
  switches to `sb_v_double_arrow` during drags, matching native's
  SetCapture+SetCursor+ReleaseCapture drag window; the K2 overlay's
  resize-handle cursors belong to the screen-capture selection feature, not
  to the translate-selection feature, and the port's OCR region overlay
  (K2) is a separate unfinished surface.
